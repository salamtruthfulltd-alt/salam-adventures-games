For parents and carers

Salam Adventures is designed to make practice feel calmer, clearer and more encouraging. Use short sessions, follow the child’s interests and celebrate effort as much as correct answers.

Helpful approach: choose one activity, keep the goal small, offer breaks and finish while the experience still feels positive.
