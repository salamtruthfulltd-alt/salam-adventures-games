There is no single right starting point. Pick an activity that feels achievable today and build confidence one small success at a time.

For parents and carers

Supportive ideas for calmer, happier learning

You know your child best. Use these activities flexibly, keep expectations kind and stop before learning becomes overwhelming.

Keep sessions short

Five or ten positive minutes can build more confidence than pushing through tiredness.

Offer choice and control

Let your child choose between two activities, select colours or decide whether to print or play.

Make breaks part of learning

Movement, quiet time, a drink or sensory support can help children regulate and return ready to learn.

Model without taking over

Show one example, think aloud and then give space. Gentle prompts support independence.

Repeat without apology

Repetition builds familiarity and security. Returning to a favourite activity is valuable practice.

Notice the whole child

Learning includes communication, confidence, creativity, emotional regulation and everyday independence.

A gentle reminder: activities on Salam Adventures are educational resources, not assessments or medical advice. Adapt them to your child and offer suitable supervision.

About Salam Adventures

Salam Adventures is a free family learning space where faith, imagination and education come together. It was created to make useful activities easier to find and to help children feel capable, included and excited to learn.
Helpful answers

Questions parents often ask

Are the games and generators free?

Yes. Activities are available for personal and educational use unless a page clearly states otherwise.

Are the resources suitable for children with additional needs?

Many activities use clear visuals, repetition, adjustable levels and short tasks that may support a wide range of learners.

How long should a child use an activity?

There is no fixed time. A short, positive session is often best.
